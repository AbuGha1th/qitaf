# C. Long lesson → series of shorts
Most posts come from long scholar lessons (not the user's own filming). The user has permission to edit and share them. Shorts are the main output; 1–2 original reels and 1 carousel per week are the exception.

1. Transcribe whole lesson; list every *self-contained benefit* (فائدة) with timestamps, a one-line hook and a score (hook strength, completeness, emotional/practical value, share-ability). Present the top 6–10; user approves.
2. Each short = one benefit. **Hard cap: 2:59 (179 s) per short.** Anything longer is cut or split into a series part.
3. **Hook = the strongest moment, moved to the front.** Pull a good part of the lesson (often the punchline, which may sit later in the original) to second 0, then play the clip from its setup. The on-screen hook line is written by hand from `ig-reel/hooks.json` formulas, Arabic only.
4. Name them as a numbered series: «فوائد من درس … (١)» — series drive follows and binge-watching.
5. Pick per short: talking-head (B) if the speaker is filmed and expressive, else voice→visuals (A) using the lesson audio.
6. **CTA end card (every short):** a closing line with subtitles on a designed background (paper or night, per §2 of SKILL.md), then the outro (E). One action per card, rotated across the series so it doesn't feel robotic. Default set:
   - «اترك تعليقا بما استفدت»
   - «شارك هذه الفائدة مع من تحب، فالدال على خير كفاعله»
   - «ما الموضوع الذي تحب أن نتناوله في الحلقة القادمة؟»
   Arabic only. The CTA line is the last thing said before the outro.
7. Subtitles: simple, Thmanyah Sans Medium, one line, lower third. No highlight pill on subtitles; the pill stays for the key phrase in talking-head shots only.
8. Source credit: the scholar's name appears in the lower third in gold for the first 3 s, and again on the end card.
9. Refinements over the first version: tighter first 2 s, consistent top title bar across the series, cover frame designed for the grid (same layout each episode, number big).
10. From each long lesson also produce 1 carousel summarising the benefits (D).

**Open, to confirm with the user before the first render:**
- Screen placement: written in `placement.md`. Confirm the open questions there (highlight pill colour, and whether the zones apply to every format).
- Whether the subtitles should carry the highlight pill (default above: no).
