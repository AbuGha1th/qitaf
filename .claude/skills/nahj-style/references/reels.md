# B. Talking-head reel (filmed lesson)
- Plan 5–6 self-contained segments (complete idea, strong open, ends on a conclusion) with start/end lines + hook title. **User approves before cutting.**
- Cut silences (silencedetect) + mistakes; crop ~608×1080 around median face → 1080×1920; crop out burned-in banners; skip host intros.
- Grade: slight warm/teal grade + vignette so footage matches the brand.
- Captions: Thmanyah Sans Medium, 2–5 words/line, lower third, small; key phrase gets the yellow pill + gold underline; synced via forced alignment.
- Comparison cut-aways: when the speaker compares, cut to a paper background with the two terms (sage vs ember, Thmanyah Serif Display), audio continues, then back.
- Ayah/hadith said in the clip → full-screen text moment as in visuals.md §6–7.
- Lower-third: speaker name with title (e.g. «الشيخ عبد الله الإدريسي») in gold, appears in first 3 s, returns briefly mid-way.
- Ending: speaker card (paper bg, ink text, gold accent) → outro.
- Length: 30–75 s best for reach; up to 3 min only when the idea needs it.
